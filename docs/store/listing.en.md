## App Store (iOS)

**Name**  
Kuran Yolu (10)

**Subtitle**  
Quran, Prayer & Learning (24)

**Promotional Text**  
Read the Quran, study Arabic, take lesson quizzes, follow prayer times and track your hatim in one app. (103)

**Description**

Read, learn and build your daily practice with Kuran Yolu. Explore the Quran alongside lessons, prayer tools and resources for reflection.

READ AND EXPLORE  
Read the Quran in a mushaf view with tajweed colors. Explore verses with word-by-word morphology, roots and tafsir. The English interface uses M. M. Pickthall's 1930 English translation of the Quran.

LEARN AT YOUR PACE  
Study structured lessons and test your understanding with quizzes. Explore Elifba lessons for learning Arabic letters and reading.

SUPPORT YOUR PRACTICE  
Check prayer times, set notifications and use the qibla compass. Prayer times are also calculated for your location abroad. Track your progress through a hatim, save favorites and keep notes.

FIND MORE TO READ  
Browse duas, hadith, seerah, stories of the prophets and worship guides.

Some content and tools are available offline; others require an internet connection. English lessons, tafsir and other content are AI-assisted translations of the app's Turkish content. (942)

**Title**  
Kuran Yolu – Learn Islam (24)

**Short description**

**Full description**

Kuran Yolu brings Quran reading, learning and daily practice together in a mobile app.

Read the Quran in a mushaf view with tajweed colors. Explore verses through word-by-word morphology, roots and tafsir. In the English interface, read M. M. Pickthall's 1930 English translation of the Quran.

Work through structured lessons and quizzes, and explore Elifba lessons for learning Arabic letters and reading. Track a hatim, save favorites and keep notes as you read.

Check prayer times, set notifications and find the qibla with the compass. Prayer times are also calculated for your location abroad. Browse duas, hadith, seerah, stories of the prophets and worship guides. On Android, add a home screen widget featuring verses of command.

WHY KURAN YOLU?
Ad-free. No advertising ID. No precise location permission. Optional backup of your favorites, notes and progress. Dark mode.

Some content and tools are available offline; others require an internet connection. English lessons, tafsir and other content are AI-assisted translations of the app's Turkish content. (868)
