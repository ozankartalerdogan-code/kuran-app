**App Review Notes** (Guideline 4.2 itirazı — App Store Connect alanı DÜZ METİN; `> ` işaretlerini atıp yapıştır.)

> Kuran Yolu is a daily worship and learning utility, not a static book or web wrapper. No account or login is required, and there are no ads or in-app purchases. After a short welcome screen, the app opens in English on any device not set to Turkish (manual switch: More > Language).
>
> Device-native and interactive tools:
> - Prayer times and local notifications: Home shows today's prayer times with a live countdown to the next prayer; tap the city name to search and choose another city. The bell icon (or More > Notifications) schedules up to 7 days of local prayer alerts, plus an optional "Daily Verse & Dua" notification.
> - Qibla compass: Worship > Qibla (or Home > Qibla) uses the device compass and approximate location to point to the Kaaba, with haptic feedback when aligned.
> - Lessons and quizzes (offline): Learn > Lessons has 16 bundled lessons in swipeable card view (Read All switches to a scrolling view); tap Start Quiz at the end of a lesson.
> - Arabic Alphabet (Elif-Ba): 9 lessons with letter audio, Practice, Listen (on multi-letter lessons such as Lesson 1), Quiz and mistake review. Lesson data and audio are downloaded on first use, so this section needs an internet connection.
> - Tasbih counter: Worship > Tasbih (or Home > Tasbih) is a tap-to-count bead ring with haptic feedback, round tracking and custom targets saved on the device.
> - Khatm tracker: Quran > Khatm (or the bookmark icon in Read the Quran): tap "+ Start a New Khatm", then mark any of the 30 juz as read and jump to its first page.
> - Offline Quran: Quran > Read the Quran opens the bundled 604-page Arabic mushaf with tajweed colors and works in airplane mode. Tap a verse > View Details (or use Quran > List of Surahs) for the bundled M. M. Pickthall (1930) English translation.
> - Verse tools: on a verse's details screen, Words and Roots show word-by-word morphology (loaded online), the share icon creates a verse image card, the heart saves it to More > My Favorites, and Personal Note saves a note (More > My Notes).
> - Prayer widget: shows the next prayer time and a countdown on the Home Screen (small and medium) and on the Lock Screen. Long-press the Home Screen, tap Edit > Add Widget and search "Kuran Yolu".
>
> Suggested 60-second tour:
> 1. Home: see the prayer countdown, tap the bell to turn on notifications, then open Qibla.
> 2. Learn > Lessons: open a lesson, swipe the cards, tap Start Quiz on the last card.
> 3. Worship > Tasbih: tap the bead ring to count.
> 4. Quran > Read the Quran: swipe pages (works in airplane mode), tap a verse > View Details, then try Words, share and the heart.
> 5. Quran > Khatm: start a khatm and mark a juz.
>
> Also included: 89 duas, 219 hadiths, 99 Names of Allah and 25 prophet stories.
>
> Privacy: location is requested only as approximate location, for prayer times and Qibla. Analytics (Firebase) and crash reports (Sentry) are anonymous; no tracking. An optional anonymous cloud backup (Firebase anonymous sign-in) keeps favorites, notes and progress, and can be deleted at any time under More > Privacy Policy.

Tafsir, Qibla, Duas & Lessons (29)

**Promotional Text**  
Read the Quran word by word, learn Islam with short lessons and quizzes, see prayer times on your Home Screen widget and find the qibla. Free, with no ads. (155)

**Description**

Kuran Yolu is a simple, ad-free app for anyone who wants to read the Quran, understand it and learn Islam step by step. No account needed.

QURAN AND READING
- All 114 surahs: Arabic text with M. M. Pickthall's English translation (1930)
- Word analysis: the root, meaning and morphology of every word
- Root explorer: a word's derivatives and where they appear in the Quran
- Tafsir: Elmalılı Hamdi Yazır's commentary on all 114 surahs in a simplified form
- Mushaf page view and color-coded tajweed reading
- Smart search across words, translation and themes

LEARN
- Card-based lessons: 16 topics, more than 260 lesson cards and a quiz in every lesson
- Topics include salah (prayer), wudu, fasting, zakat, hajj and umrah, qurbani, the articles of faith, good character, tajweed, the life of the Prophet (seerah), the lives of the Companions, stories of the prophets and more
- Elif-Ba: lessons for learning the Arabic alphabet, letter quizzes and audio

WORSHIP GUIDES
- Prayer guide: how to pray step by step, prayer supplications, short surahs and special prayers
- Worship guide: the pillars of Islam and faith, wudu, ghusl, tayammum, fasting, zakat, hajj and qurbani

DUAS AND DHIKR
- 89 duas in 23 categories
- Spiritual refuge: duas for protection and healing, the Greatest Name and the Sakinah verses
- Digital tasbih counter

ISLAMIC KNOWLEDGE
- Asma ul-Husna: the 99 Names of Allah with Arabic script, meaning and root
- Seerah: the life of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him)
- Stories of 25 prophets
- 219 selected hadiths in 27 topics, mostly from Bukhari and Muslim
- Blessed days and nights and their virtues

TOOLS
- Qibla compass
- Prayer times for your location, anywhere in the world
- Prayer time notifications (each with a verse) and optional daily verse and dua notifications
- Verse of the Day and Dua of the Day
- Prayer times widget: the next prayer and a countdown on your Home Screen and Lock Screen

PERSONAL
- Favorites and personal notes
- Khatm (hatim) tracking and a daily reading streak
- Your progress is kept safe with an anonymous cloud backup that you can delete from inside the app at any time

WHY KURAN YOLU?
- No ads
- Privacy-friendly: no advertising ID, no tracking; location is used only approximately, for prayer times and the qibla
- Much of the app works offline (lessons, tafsir and duas are stored on the device)
- Clean, modern interface in English and Turkish

English lessons, tafsir and other content are AI-assisted translations of the app's Turkish content.

May Allah make this app a means of bringing us closer to the Quran and to knowledge.

**Title**  
Kuran Yolu: Quran, Prayer Time (30)

**Short description**  
Ad-free offline Quran, word-by-word roots, prayer times, Qibla & Arabic letters (79)

**Full description**

Kuran Yolu brings Quran reading, learning and daily practice together in a mobile app. It is free and ad-free, and the full 604-page tajweed mushaf works offline.

Read the Quran in a mushaf view with tajweed colors. Explore verses through word-by-word morphology, roots and tafsir. In the English interface, read M. M. Pickthall's 1930 English translation of the Quran.

Work through structured lessons and quizzes, and explore Elifba lessons for learning Arabic letters and reading, with Listen & Choose practice, review of your mistakes and lesson badges. Track a hatim, save favorites and keep notes as you read.

Check prayer times from Türkiye's Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet): find your city automatically or pick it manually anywhere in the world. Set prayer notifications that arrive on time abroad and across time zones, and find the qibla with the compass. Use the tasbih counter and browse duas, hadith, seerah, stories of the prophets and worship guides. On Android, add home screen widgets for prayer times, the next prayer and verses of command.

WHY KURAN YOLU?
Ad-free. No advertising ID. No precise location permission. Anonymous backup of your favorites, notes and progress, which you can delete in the app. Dark mode.

Some content and tools are available offline; others require an internet connection. English lessons, tafsir and other content are AI-assisted translations of the app's Turkish content. (1435)
