**App Review Notes** (taslak — gönderimden önce ekran adlarını TestFlight derlemesinde doğrula)

> No account or login is required. The app works offline for reading; prayer times need a one-time network fetch.
>
> Suggested 60-second tour (set the device language to English, or use More → Language):
> 1. Quran tab → "Read Quran": the full 604-page mushaf is bundled and works in airplane mode, with tajweed colors and hatim (completion) tracking.
> 2. Tap any verse → verse screen: word-by-word morphology, Arabic roots, tafsir, and a shareable verse image card.
> 3. Learn tab: structured lessons with quizzes, and "Arabic Alphabet (Elif-Ba)" for learning Arabic letters step by step.
> 4. Worship tab: Qibla compass (uses the device compass and approximate location), tasbih counter, and daily prayer times with optional local notifications.
>
> Location is requested only as approximate location for prayer times and Qibla. Analytics (Firebase) and crash reports (Sentry) are anonymous; no tracking, no ads, no in-app purchases.

**Full description**

Kuran Yolu brings Quran reading, learning and daily practice together in a mobile app.

Read the Quran in a mushaf view with tajweed colors. Explore verses through word-by-word morphology, roots and tafsir. In the English interface, read M. M. Pickthall's 1930 English translation of the Quran.

Work through structured lessons and quizzes, and explore Elifba lessons for learning Arabic letters and reading, with listening practice, review of your mistakes and lesson badges. Track a hatim, save favorites and keep notes as you read.

Check prayer times from Türkiye's Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet): find your city automatically or pick it manually anywhere in the world. Set prayer notifications that arrive on time abroad and across time zones, and find the qibla with the compass. Browse duas, hadith, seerah, stories of the prophets and worship guides. On Android, add a home screen widget featuring verses of command.

WHY KURAN YOLU?
Ad-free. No advertising ID. No precise location permission. Optional backup of your favorites, notes and progress. Dark mode.

Some content and tools are available offline; others require an internet connection. English lessons, tafsir and other content are AI-assisted translations of the app's Turkish content. (868)
